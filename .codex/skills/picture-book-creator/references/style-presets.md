# 绘本风格预设库

每种风格包含：适用场景、推荐年龄档位、完整的图像 Prompt 前缀模板。
阶段 1 收集需求时展示风格选项，阶段 5 生成 style.md 时参考对应模板。

---

## 1. 经典水彩 (Classic Watercolor)

**代表作品**：《猜猜我有多爱你》《彼得兔》系列风格
**适用场景**：温馨亲情、自然探索、睡前故事
**推荐档位**：全年龄，尤其 BABY / TODDLER
**情绪基调**：温馨治愈

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Soft watercolor illustration style, visible gentle brushstrokes with wet-on-wet blending,
delicate color washes with naturally bleeding edges, pastel and muted warm tones,
light ivory/cream paper texture showing through, minimal outlines with color defining form,
soft diffused natural lighting, dreamy atmospheric quality,
children's picture book illustration, gentle and tender mood
```

**负面约束**：
```
no sharp edges, no solid flat fills, no digital perfection, no harsh shadows,
no neon colors, no heavy black outlines
```

---

## 2. 蜡笔涂鸦 (Crayon Texture)

**代表作品**：《蜡笔小黑》《Harold and the Purple Crayon》风格
**适用场景**：创意想象、色彩认知、手工感故事
**推荐档位**：BABY / TODDLER
**情绪基调**：轻松幽默

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Crayon-textured illustration style, visible waxy crayon strokes with grain texture,
bold and vibrant primary colors, slightly uneven coloring with childlike charm,
thick rounded strokes, simple shapes with soft edges,
white or light paper background showing through crayon texture,
warm cheerful lighting, playful and joyful atmosphere,
children's picture book illustration, hand-drawn crayon art feel
```

**负面约束**：
```
no smooth gradients, no photorealistic rendering, no thin precise lines,
no metallic or glossy effects, no complex shading
```

---

## 3. 拼贴艺术 (Collage Art)

**代表作品**：Eric Carle《好饿的毛毛虫》《棕色的熊》风格
**适用场景**：动物认知、颜色学习、感官启蒙
**推荐档位**：BABY / TODDLER
**情绪基调**：轻松幽默、冒险好奇

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Paper collage illustration style, layered cut-paper textures with visible edges,
hand-painted tissue paper pieces assembled into shapes, bold saturated colors,
textured surfaces with paint strokes visible on paper pieces,
simple graphic compositions with strong silhouettes,
flat perspective with slight dimensional overlap of paper layers,
bright and vivid, high contrast between elements and background,
children's picture book illustration, handcrafted paper art aesthetic
```

**负面约束**：
```
no smooth digital rendering, no realistic textures, no gradients,
no thin outlines, no perspective depth, no photographic elements
```

---

## 4. 手绘线条淡彩 (Line and Wash)

**代表作品**：E.H. Shepard《小熊维尼》、Quentin Blake 插画风格
**适用场景**：冒险故事、幽默故事、经典童话
**推荐档位**：PRESCHOOL / EARLY-READER
**情绪基调**：轻松幽默、冒险好奇

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Hand-drawn ink line illustration with light watercolor wash,
expressive loose pen strokes with personality and movement,
thin to medium black ink outlines with natural variation in line weight,
selective color washes — not every area filled, some areas left as white space,
spontaneous and sketchy quality with confident linework,
light and airy composition with plenty of breathing room,
subtle warm color palette applied loosely over ink drawing,
children's picture book illustration, classic storybook charm
```

**负面约束**：
```
no heavy coloring, no solid fills everywhere, no digital precision,
no thick outlines, no dark moody tones, no photorealistic rendering
```

---

## 5. 日系柔和 (Japanese Soft / Kawaii)

**代表作品**：岩村和朗《14只老鼠》、林明子《第一次上街买东西》风格
**适用场景**：日常生活、家庭亲情、自然四季、成长故事
**推荐档位**：TODDLER / PRESCHOOL
**情绪基调**：温馨治愈

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Soft Japanese illustration style, delicate and detailed with gentle brushwork,
warm earthy natural color palette with muted greens, browns, and soft yellows,
fine precise linework with rounded soft character forms,
rich environmental details — plants, textures, small objects carefully rendered,
warm golden-hour or soft daylight lighting with gentle shadows,
cozy intimate atmosphere, slightly overhead or eye-level perspective,
characters with large expressive eyes and round proportions,
children's picture book illustration, tender and heartwarming Japanese art style
```

**负面约束**：
```
no harsh colors, no angular sharp features, no dark shadows,
no abstract compositions, no flat vector style, no exaggerated cartoon proportions
```

---

## 6. 北欧简约 (Nordic Minimal)

**代表作品**：Tove Jansson《姆明》、现代斯堪的纳维亚绘本风格
**适用场景**：哲理小品、自然探索、情感认知
**推荐档位**：PRESCHOOL / EARLY-READER
**情绪基调**：温馨治愈、冒险好奇

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Nordic minimalist illustration style, clean simple compositions with bold graphic shapes,
limited refined color palette — 3 to 5 colors per spread,
muted Scandinavian tones: slate blue, sage green, warm grey, mustard yellow, soft white,
generous use of negative space and white backgrounds,
simple geometric character forms with minimal facial features but strong expression,
flat or subtly textured fills with occasional fine line details,
calm contemplative mood with quiet elegance,
children's picture book illustration, modern Scandinavian design aesthetic
```

**负面约束**：
```
no busy cluttered compositions, no bright neon colors, no complex realistic textures,
no heavy outlines, no dramatic lighting, no ornate decorative elements
```

## 7. 扁平矢量 (Flat Vector / Graphic)

**代表作品**：Leo Lionni《小蓝和小黄》《田鼠阿佛》风格
**适用场景**：概念学习、社交情感、寓言故事
**推荐档位**：BABY / TODDLER / PRESCHOOL
**情绪基调**：轻松幽默

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Flat vector illustration style, bold geometric shapes with clean edges,
bright solid color fills with no gradients, strong graphic compositions,
simplified character forms using basic shapes — circles, ovals, rectangles,
high contrast between foreground elements and solid color backgrounds,
no outlines or very minimal thin outlines, color-blocked design,
modern and playful with strong visual rhythm,
children's picture book illustration, contemporary graphic design aesthetic
```

**负面约束**：
```
no gradients, no textures, no realistic rendering, no complex details,
no shadow effects, no 3D appearance, no hand-drawn irregularity
```

---

## 8. 复古卡通 (Retro Storybook)

**代表作品**：《好奇的乔治》《大象巴巴》《玛德琳》风格
**适用场景**：冒险探索、城市/旅行、幽默故事
**推荐档位**：PRESCHOOL / EARLY-READER
**情绪基调**：轻松幽默、冒险好奇

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Retro vintage storybook illustration style, mid-century modern aesthetic,
bold confident ink outlines with hand-drawn character,
limited but vivid color palette — often red, yellow, blue with earthy accents,
slightly naive and charming drawing style with personality,
crosshatching and simple shading techniques,
dynamic compositions with characters in action and movement,
warm nostalgic atmosphere with timeless appeal,
children's picture book illustration, classic retro storybook art
```

**负面约束**：
```
no digital smoothness, no photorealistic rendering, no complex gradients,
no modern flat design, no anime style, no dark gothic tones
```

## 9. 数字绘画 (Digital Painting)

**代表作品**：现代获奖绘本、Pixar 概念艺术风格
**适用场景**：奇幻冒险、情感深度、视觉震撼型故事
**推荐档位**：PRESCHOOL / EARLY-READER
**情绪基调**：冒险好奇、温馨治愈

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Digital painting illustration style, rich detailed rendering with soft blending,
vibrant cinematic color palette with atmospheric depth,
painterly brushstrokes visible but refined, smooth character forms with clear features,
dramatic but child-friendly lighting — warm rim light, soft ambient glow,
layered compositions with foreground, midground, and background depth,
expressive large eyes on characters, appealing rounded proportions,
magical dreamy atmosphere with rich environmental storytelling,
children's picture book illustration, high-quality digital art with storybook warmth
```

**负面约束**：
```
no flat vector style, no rough sketchy lines, no photorealistic humans,
no dark horror lighting, no overly complex mechanical details
```

## 10. 民间艺术 (Folk Art)

**代表作品**：各国民间故事绘本、非洲/拉美/东欧风格绘本
**适用场景**：文化故事、民俗传说、多元文化主题
**推荐档位**：PRESCHOOL / EARLY-READER
**情绪基调**：温馨治愈、冒险好奇

**Prompt 模板**：
```
Folk art illustration style, decorative patterned surfaces with cultural motifs,
bold warm earthy color palette — terracotta, ochre, deep blue, forest green, gold,
stylized non-realistic proportions with expressive faces,
hand-painted texture with visible brushwork and pattern fills,
symmetrical and decorative compositions with ornamental borders,
flat perspective with layered elements, rich textile-like surface quality,
warm celebratory atmosphere with storytelling tradition,
children's picture book illustration, handcrafted folk art aesthetic
```

**负面约束**：
```
no photorealistic rendering, no smooth digital gradients, no minimalist design,
no anime style, no cold color palettes, no sterile compositions
```
